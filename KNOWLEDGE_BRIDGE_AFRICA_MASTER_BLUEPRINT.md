# Knowledge Bridge Africa: Master Blueprint (Project Bible)

## 1. Executive Summary

### Vision
To build a continental digital bridge that harmonizes Africa’s rich oral histories and indigenous knowledge with global academic science, fostering a unified repository of human wisdom for sustainable innovation.

### Mission
To ethically record, structure, validate, and scale indigenous science, traditional medical knowledge, and ecological management practices across Africa, ensuring intellectual property rights are protected and cultural custodians are respected.

### Core Philosophy: Complementary Dual-Integrity
Global academic science and indigenous oral knowledge are non-hierarchical, complementary pillars of human understanding. The platform does not seek to subsume oral history into Western scientific paradigms, but rather to map correlations, build conceptual bridges, and validate empirical results mutually.

### Long-term Goals
- **Scale:** 10M+ curated multi-modal knowledge records.
- **Reach:** 500+ partnering African universities and 54 African nations.
- **Sovereignty:** Model-agnostic architecture to protect intellectual assets from external vendor shifts.
- **Accessibility:** 100% African language coverage (2,000+ languages).

### Stakeholders
- **Primary:** Indigenous custodians, elders, traditional agriculturalists, academic researchers, and students.
- **Secondary:** Global scholars, pharmaceutical researchers, environmental NGOs, and national policymakers.

## 3. Information Architecture

### Navigation Structure
The platform is anchored by a persistent, high-contrast responsive navigation system, transitioning between:
- **Home:** Personalized dashboard and global impact metrics.
- **Knowledge Bridge:** AI-first interaction layer for synthesis.
- **Knowledge Library:** Structured repository mapped to taxonomy.
- **Research:** Academic peer-review portal and datasets.
- **Community:** Regional hubs and elder verification circles.
- **Innovation Hub:** Commercialization and patent tracking.

### Knowledge Taxonomy
Poly-hierarchical domains:
- **Life Sciences:** Ethnobotany, Ecology, Human Health, Maternal Wisdom.
- **Physical Sciences:** Indigenous Engineering, Sustainable Metallurgy.
- **Linguistics:** Oral Traditions, Proverbial Logic, Dialect Mapping.
- **Environmental:** Climate Resilience, Water Sovereignty, Biodiversity.

## 4. User Roles & Governance

### User Roles (RBAC)
- **Guest:** Read-only access to public content.
- **Student:** Bookmarks, learning tracking, limited AI queries.
- **Researcher:** Uploads papers/datasets, verified status for peer review.
- **Community Elder:** Submits oral traditions, verification authority for traditional claims.
- **Moderator:** Dispute handling and flag management.
- **Administrator:** Full configuration and user management.

### Ethical Principles (The Governance Charter)
- **Prior Informed Consent (PIC):** Mandatory recorded consent for all indigenous knowledge.
- **Bio-piracy Prevention:** Zero-knowledge encryption for sensitive pharmacological recipes.
- **Absolute Transparency:** Every claim must link to a verifiable source; no anonymous facts.

## 5. Knowledge Architecture

### Core Knowledge Object (CKO) v2.0
The irreducible unit of information, a polymorphic container spanning:
- **Identifiers:** Unique Canonical UUID, Title, Summary.
- **Knowledge Core:** Markdown description, Scientific Explanation, Indigenous Wisdom.
- **Trust Metrics:** Evidence Level (1-5), Confidence Score (0-1).
- **Taxonomy & Geo:** Spatiotemporal coordinate tags, ISO Language codes.
- **References:** DOIs, Oral Source Citations, Media Assets.

### Relationship Graph (The Bridge Graph)
Interconnected nodes linked via specific relation types:
- **Supports:** Evidence or validation (Science -> Tradition).
- **Contradicts:** Refutation or opposing evidence.
- **Is Translation Of:** Cross-language mapping.
- **Is Studied By:** Links knowledge to institutions.

## 6. AI Engine Architecture (High Level)

### Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
Ensures zero hallucinations through traceable citations and confidence scoring. AI evaluates prompts against source credibility and consensus density.

### Multilingual Intelligence
- **Context-Aware Translation:** Preserves original nuance (e.g., clinical vs spiritual context).
- **Terminology Protection:** "No-Translate" registry for sacred terms.
- **Voice Interaction:** Speech-to-Text for oral history contribution.
